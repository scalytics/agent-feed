Scalytics product feeds

These are the public OpenAI product feeds for ChatGPT, built from products.yaml in the scalytics/agent-feed repository. There is one feed per product or catalog. Use a parquet URL as the Hosted URL in the OpenAI Business Hub. Parquet is the format OpenAI prefers.

The original root files remain the Lascaris feed:

  • products.parquet
  • products.csv
  • products.jsonl.gz
